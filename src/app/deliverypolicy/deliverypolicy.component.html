🚚 Delivery Policy

At Pizza Boyz, we ensure your pizza arrives hot, fresh, and on time!

  • ✔️ Orders are delivered within 30-45 minutes depending on location.

  • ✔️ Delivery is available within a 5km radius of our stores.

  • ✔️ A small delivery fee may apply based on distance.

  • ✔️ We ensure all pizzas are packed safely to retain freshness and warmth.
  • ✔️ In case of delays due to traffic or weather, our team will notify you.

⚠️ Please ensure your contact details are correct to avoid delivery issues.
